Attendance Note – Meeting with Margaret Lau

Duration: Approximately one hour

Met with Margaret Lau for an initial fact-finding and review meeting concerning the Lakeside Family Trust. The purpose of the meeting was to establish an accurate understanding of the current family and beneficiary position before any further review or substantive advice is undertaken.

At the outset, Margaret Lau explained that she wanted to make sure that the basic factual position concerning the Lakeside Family Trust was recorded correctly. It was explained that the meeting would therefore focus on gathering and confirming the relevant background information rather than reaching conclusions about the trust or giving substantive advice. Margaret Lau confirmed that this approach was consistent with what she wanted from the meeting.

The scope of the discussion was explained. In particular, it was noted that the immediate purpose was to identify the relevant individuals, confirm their relationship to one another where necessary, and confirm the persons connected with the Lakeside Family Trust in the capacities relevant to the present review. It was also explained that recording a fact during this meeting should not be treated as a conclusion about matters that had not yet been reviewed.

Margaret Lau was asked first to confirm her own connection with the Lakeside Family Trust. She confirmed that she is the settlor of the Lakeside Family Trust. This was discussed as part of establishing the basic background to the trust and was recorded as part of the factual information for the review.

The discussion then moved to Margaret Lau's immediate family position insofar as it was relevant to understanding the information she was providing. Margaret Lau confirmed that she and Henry Lau are spouses. She was then asked about the children of the family so that the relationships between the individuals being discussed were clear. Margaret Lau confirmed that Margaret Lau and Henry Lau are the parents of Daniel Lau and of Elena Lau. She further confirmed that Daniel Lau and Elena Lau are siblings.

This information was repeated back to Margaret Lau to ensure that it had been understood correctly. Margaret Lau confirmed that the description of the family position was accurate. No wider family information was required for the limited purpose of the present meeting, and the discussion remained focused on the individuals relevant to the review.

The meeting then returned to the Lakeside Family Trust. It was explained that, for the purposes of the initial factual review, it was important to distinguish between a person's family relationship and that person's separate connection with the trust. Margaret Lau was therefore asked to identify the beneficiaries of the Lakeside Family Trust relevant to the present discussion rather than relying on assumptions arising from the family information already recorded.

Margaret Lau confirmed that Daniel Lau is a beneficiary of the Lakeside Family Trust. She was asked to confirm that this was a trust relationship in addition to the family information already discussed, and she confirmed that this was correct.

Margaret Lau also confirmed that Priya Nair is a beneficiary of the Lakeside Family Trust. No assumption was made or discussion recorded as to any relationship between Priya Nair and Margaret Lau, Henry Lau, Daniel Lau or Elena Lau. The purpose of mentioning Priya Nair during the meeting was limited to recording Priya Nair's position as a beneficiary of the Lakeside Family Trust.

The meeting then turned to the trustee position. Margaret Lau confirmed that Henry Lau is a trustee of the Lakeside Family Trust. She was asked to confirm that this was a separate role from the family information already discussed, and she confirmed that this was correct, and that no other person discussed during the meeting held the office of trustee.

The beneficiary information was then read back in summary form to avoid any misunderstanding. Margaret Lau confirmed that the factual position to be recorded for the purposes of the initial review was that Daniel Lau and Priya Nair are beneficiaries of the Lakeside Family Trust.

Margaret Lau asked what would happen after the initial fact-finding stage. It was explained that the next stage of a review would ordinarily depend on the scope of the work ultimately required and on the information available for review. The present meeting was not intended to determine matters that had not yet been examined. It was therefore important to keep the factual information confirmed during the meeting separate from any later analysis.

It was also explained that the current discussion did not involve a review of the detailed terms or operation of the Lakeside Family Trust. No conclusions were reached during the meeting about matters outside the factual family and trust relationships expressly discussed. Margaret Lau indicated that she understood that distinction.

Margaret Lau asked whether any further substantive conclusions could be drawn from the information confirmed during the meeting alone. It was explained that the purpose of the meeting was to establish a reliable starting point and that further review would be required before addressing matters beyond that limited factual exercise. Margaret Lau agreed that the immediate priority was for the basic information to be accurately recorded.

The information gathered during the meeting was then reviewed again for consistency. Margaret Lau confirmed that she is the settlor of the Lakeside Family Trust. She confirmed that she and Henry Lau are spouses and that Margaret Lau and Henry Lau are the parents of Daniel Lau and of Elena Lau, and that Daniel Lau and Elena Lau are siblings. She also confirmed that Daniel Lau and Priya Nair are beneficiaries of the Lakeside Family Trust. She further confirmed that Henry Lau is a trustee of the Lakeside Family Trust.

Care was taken during the recap to distinguish between the family information and the trust information. In particular, the fact that Daniel Lau and Elena Lau are children of Margaret Lau and Henry Lau, and that they are siblings, was recorded separately from the fact that Daniel Lau is a beneficiary of the Lakeside Family Trust. Priya Nair's inclusion in the discussion was confined to Priya Nair's status as a beneficiary of the Lakeside Family Trust. Henry Lau's position as a trustee of the Lakeside Family Trust was recorded separately from his family relationships and from the beneficiary information.

Margaret Lau was asked whether the summary accurately reflected the information she had intended to provide during the meeting. She confirmed that it did.

It was agreed that the information confirmed during the meeting would form the factual starting point for any further review. Any later consideration of matters not addressed during the meeting would require separate review rather than assumption. No substantive conclusion was reached about any matter beyond the factual information recorded above.

The meeting concluded with a final confirmation that the present exercise was intended to establish an accurate record of the relevant family and beneficiary position before further work is undertaken. Margaret Lau confirmed that this remained her objective.
