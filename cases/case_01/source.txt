Attendance Note – Meeting with Alice Chen

Duration: Approximately one hour

Met with Alice Chen for an initial fact-finding and review meeting concerning the Evergreen Family Trust. The purpose of the meeting was to establish an accurate understanding of the current family and beneficiary position before any further review or substantive advice is undertaken.

At the outset, Alice Chen explained that she wanted to make sure that the basic factual position concerning the Evergreen Family Trust was recorded correctly. It was explained that the meeting would therefore focus on gathering and confirming the relevant background information rather than reaching conclusions about the trust or giving substantive advice. Alice Chen confirmed that this approach was consistent with what she wanted from the meeting.

The scope of the discussion was explained. In particular, it was noted that the immediate purpose was to identify the relevant individuals, confirm their relationship to one another where necessary, and confirm the persons connected with the Evergreen Family Trust in the capacities relevant to the present review. It was also explained that recording a fact during this meeting should not be treated as a conclusion about matters that had not yet been reviewed.

Alice Chen was asked first to confirm her own connection with the Evergreen Family Trust. She confirmed that she is the settlor of the Evergreen Family Trust. This was discussed as part of establishing the basic background to the trust and was recorded as part of the factual information for the review.

The discussion then moved to Alice Chen's immediate family position insofar as it was relevant to understanding the information she was providing. Alice Chen confirmed that she and David Chen are spouses. She was then asked about Bob Chen so that the relationship between the individuals being discussed was clear. Alice Chen confirmed that Alice Chen and David Chen are the parents of Bob Chen.

This information was repeated back to Alice Chen to ensure that it had been understood correctly. Alice Chen confirmed that the description of the family position was accurate. No wider family information was required for the limited purpose of the present meeting, and the discussion remained focused on the individuals relevant to the review.

The meeting then returned to the Evergreen Family Trust. It was explained that, for the purposes of the initial factual review, it was important to distinguish between a person's family relationship and that person's separate connection with the trust. Alice Chen was therefore asked to identify the beneficiaries of the Evergreen Family Trust relevant to the present discussion rather than relying on assumptions arising from the family information already recorded.

Alice Chen confirmed that Bob Chen is a beneficiary of the Evergreen Family Trust. She was asked to confirm that this was a trust relationship in addition to the family information already discussed, and she confirmed that this was correct.

Alice Chen also confirmed that Carol Wong is a beneficiary of the Evergreen Family Trust. No assumption was made or discussion recorded as to any relationship between Carol Wong and Alice Chen, David Chen or Bob Chen. The purpose of mentioning Carol Wong during the meeting was limited to recording Carol Wong's position as a beneficiary of the Evergreen Family Trust.

The beneficiary information was then read back in summary form to avoid any misunderstanding. Alice Chen confirmed that the factual position to be recorded for the purposes of the initial review was that Bob Chen and Carol Wong are beneficiaries of the Evergreen Family Trust.

Alice Chen asked what would happen after the initial fact-finding stage. It was explained that the next stage of a review would ordinarily depend on the scope of the work ultimately required and on the information available for review. The present meeting was not intended to determine matters that had not yet been examined. It was therefore important to keep the factual information confirmed during the meeting separate from any later analysis.

It was also explained that the current discussion did not involve a review of the detailed terms or operation of the Evergreen Family Trust. No conclusions were reached during the meeting about matters outside the factual family and trust relationships expressly discussed. Alice Chen indicated that she understood that distinction.

Alice Chen asked whether any further substantive conclusions could be drawn from the information confirmed during the meeting alone. It was explained that the purpose of the meeting was to establish a reliable starting point and that further review would be required before addressing matters beyond that limited factual exercise. Alice Chen agreed that the immediate priority was for the basic information to be accurately recorded.

The information gathered during the meeting was then reviewed again for consistency. Alice Chen confirmed that she is the settlor of the Evergreen Family Trust. She confirmed that she and David Chen are spouses and that Alice Chen and David Chen are the parents of Bob Chen. She also confirmed that Bob Chen and Carol Wong are beneficiaries of the Evergreen Family Trust.

Care was taken during the recap to distinguish between the family information and the trust information. In particular, the fact that Bob Chen is a child of Alice Chen and David Chen was recorded separately from the fact that Bob Chen is a beneficiary of the Evergreen Family Trust. Carol Wong's inclusion in the discussion was confined to Carol Wong's status as a beneficiary of the Evergreen Family Trust.

Alice Chen was asked whether the summary accurately reflected the information she had intended to provide during the meeting. She confirmed that it did.

It was agreed that the information confirmed during the meeting would form the factual starting point for any further review. Any later consideration of matters not addressed during the meeting would require separate review rather than assumption. No substantive conclusion was reached about any matter beyond the factual information recorded above.

The meeting concluded with a final confirmation that the present exercise was intended to establish an accurate record of the relevant family and beneficiary position before further work is undertaken. Alice Chen confirmed that this remained her objective.
