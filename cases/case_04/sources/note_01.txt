Attendance Note – Meeting with Victoria Chan

Duration: Approximately one hour

Met with Victoria Chan for an initial fact-finding and review meeting concerning the Hillcrest Family Trust. The purpose of the meeting was to establish an accurate understanding of the current family and beneficiary position before any further review or substantive advice is undertaken.

At the outset, Victoria Chan explained that she wanted to make sure that the basic factual position concerning the Hillcrest Family Trust was recorded correctly. It was explained that the meeting would therefore focus on gathering and confirming the relevant background information rather than reaching conclusions about the trust or giving substantive advice. Victoria Chan confirmed that this approach was consistent with what she wanted from the meeting.

The scope of the discussion was explained. In particular, it was noted that the immediate purpose was to identify the relevant individuals, confirm their relationship to one another where necessary, and confirm the persons connected with the Hillcrest Family Trust in the capacities relevant to the present review. It was also explained that recording a fact during this meeting should not be treated as a conclusion about matters that had not yet been reviewed.

Victoria Chan was asked first to confirm her own connection with the Hillcrest Family Trust. She confirmed that she is the settlor of the Hillcrest Family Trust. This was discussed as part of establishing the basic background to the trust and was recorded as part of the factual information for the review.

The discussion then moved to Victoria Chan's immediate family position insofar as it was relevant to understanding the information she was providing. Victoria Chan confirmed that she and Peter Chan are spouses. She was then asked about the children of the family so that the relationships between the individuals being discussed were clear. Victoria Chan confirmed that Victoria Chan and Peter Chan are the parents of Andrew Chan.

This information was repeated back to Victoria Chan to ensure that it had been understood correctly. Victoria Chan confirmed that the description of the family position was accurate. No wider family information was required for the limited purpose of the present meeting, and the discussion remained focused on the individuals relevant to the review.

The meeting then returned to the Hillcrest Family Trust. It was explained that, for the purposes of the initial factual review, it was important to distinguish between a person's family relationship and that person's separate connection with the trust. Victoria Chan was therefore asked to identify the beneficiaries of the Hillcrest Family Trust relevant to the present discussion rather than relying on assumptions arising from the family information already recorded.

Victoria Chan confirmed that Andrew Chan is a beneficiary of the Hillcrest Family Trust. She was asked to confirm that this was a trust relationship in addition to the family information already discussed, and she confirmed that this was correct.

The beneficiary information was then read back in summary form to avoid any misunderstanding. Victoria Chan confirmed that the factual position to be recorded for the purposes of the initial review was that Andrew Chan is a beneficiary of the Hillcrest Family Trust.

Victoria Chan asked what would happen after the initial fact-finding stage. It was explained that the next stage of a review would ordinarily depend on the scope of the work ultimately required and on the information available for review. The present meeting was not intended to determine matters that had not yet been examined. It was therefore important to keep the factual information confirmed during the meeting separate from any later analysis.

It was also explained that the current discussion did not involve a review of the detailed terms or operation of the Hillcrest Family Trust. No conclusions were reached during the meeting about matters outside the factual family and trust relationships expressly discussed. Victoria Chan indicated that she understood that distinction.

Victoria Chan asked whether any further substantive conclusions could be drawn from the information confirmed during the meeting alone. It was explained that the purpose of the meeting was to establish a reliable starting point and that further review would be required before addressing matters beyond that limited factual exercise. Victoria Chan agreed that the immediate priority was for the basic information to be accurately recorded.

The information gathered during the meeting was then reviewed again for consistency. Victoria Chan confirmed that she is the settlor of the Hillcrest Family Trust. She confirmed that she and Peter Chan are spouses and that Victoria Chan and Peter Chan are the parents of Andrew Chan. She also confirmed that Andrew Chan is a beneficiary of the Hillcrest Family Trust.

Care was taken during the recap to distinguish between the family information and the trust information. In particular, the fact that Andrew Chan is a child of Victoria Chan and Peter Chan was recorded separately from the fact that Andrew Chan is a beneficiary of the Hillcrest Family Trust.

Victoria Chan was asked whether the summary accurately reflected the information she had intended to provide during the meeting. She confirmed that it did.

It was agreed that the information confirmed during the meeting would form the factual starting point for any further review. Any later consideration of matters not addressed during the meeting would require separate review rather than assumption. No substantive conclusion was reached about any matter beyond the factual information recorded above.

The meeting concluded with a final confirmation that the present exercise was intended to establish an accurate record of the relevant family and beneficiary position before further work is undertaken. Victoria Chan confirmed that this remained her objective.
