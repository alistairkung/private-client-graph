Attendance Note – Meeting with Rebecca Tan

Duration: Approximately one hour

Met with Rebecca Tan for an initial fact-finding and review meeting concerning the Riverside Family Trust. The purpose of the meeting was to establish an accurate understanding of the current family and beneficiary position before any further review or substantive advice is undertaken.

At the outset, Rebecca Tan explained that she wanted to make sure that the basic factual position concerning the Riverside Family Trust was recorded correctly. It was explained that the meeting would therefore focus on gathering and confirming the relevant background information rather than reaching conclusions about the trust or giving substantive advice. Rebecca confirmed that this approach was consistent with what she wanted from the meeting.

The scope of the discussion was explained. In particular, it was noted that the immediate purpose was to identify the relevant individuals, confirm their relationship to one another where necessary, and confirm the persons connected with the Riverside Family Trust in the capacities relevant to the present review. It was also explained that recording a fact during this meeting should not be treated as a conclusion about matters that had not yet been reviewed.

Rebecca Tan was asked first to confirm her own connection with the Riverside Family Trust. She confirmed that she is the settlor of the Riverside Family Trust. This was discussed as part of establishing the basic background to the trust and was recorded as part of the factual information for the review.

The discussion then moved to Mrs Tan's immediate family position insofar as it was relevant to understanding the information she was providing. Rebecca confirmed that she and Jonathan Tan are spouses. She was then asked about the children of the family so that the relationships between the individuals being discussed were clear. Rebecca Tan confirmed that Rebecca Tan and Jonathan Tan are the parents of Michael Tan.

This information was repeated back to Rebecca to ensure that it had been understood correctly. Mrs Tan confirmed that the description of the family position was accurate. No wider family information was required for the limited purpose of the present meeting, and the discussion remained focused on the individuals relevant to the review.

The meeting then returned to the Riverside Family Trust. It was explained that, for the purposes of the initial factual review, it was important to distinguish between a person's family relationship and that person's separate connection with the trust. Rebecca Tan was therefore asked to identify the beneficiaries of the Riverside Family Trust relevant to the present discussion rather than relying on assumptions arising from the family information already recorded.

Rebecca confirmed that Michael Tan is a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. She was asked to confirm that this was a trust relationship in addition to the family information already discussed, and she confirmed that this was correct.

Mrs Tan also confirmed that Sophie Tan is a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. No assumption was made or discussion recorded as to any relationship between Sophie Tan and Rebecca Tan, Jonathan Tan or Michael Tan. The purpose of mentioning Sophie Tan during the meeting was limited to recording Sophie Tan's position as a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. Rebecca Tan further confirmed that Adrian Koh is a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. No assumption was made or discussion recorded as to any relationship between Adrian Koh and Rebecca Tan, Jonathan Tan, Michael Tan or Sophie Tan. The purpose of mentioning Adrian Koh during the meeting was limited to recording Adrian Koh's position as a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust.

The beneficiary information was then read back in summary form to avoid any misunderstanding. Rebecca confirmed that the factual position to be recorded for the purposes of the initial review was that Michael Tan, Sophie Tan and Adrian Koh are beneficiaries of the Riverside Family Trust.

Rebecca Tan asked what would happen after the initial fact-finding stage. It was explained that the next stage of a review would ordinarily depend on the scope of the work ultimately required and on the information available for review. The present meeting was not intended to determine matters that had not yet been examined. It was therefore important to keep the factual information confirmed during the meeting separate from any later analysis.

It was also explained that the current discussion did not involve a review of the detailed terms or operation of the Riverside Family Trust. No conclusions were reached during the meeting about matters outside the factual family and trust relationships expressly discussed. Mrs Tan indicated that she understood that distinction.

Mrs Tan asked whether any further substantive conclusions could be drawn from the information confirmed during the meeting alone. It was explained that the purpose of the meeting was to establish a reliable starting point and that further review would be required before addressing matters beyond that limited factual exercise. Rebecca agreed that the immediate priority was for the basic information to be accurately recorded.

The information gathered during the meeting was then reviewed again for consistency. Rebecca Tan confirmed that she is the settlor of the Riverside Family Trust. She confirmed that she and Jonathan Tan are spouses and that Rebecca Tan and Jonathan Tan are the parents of Michael Tan. She also confirmed that Michael Tan, Sophie Tan and Adrian Koh are beneficiaries of the Riverside Family Trust.

Care was taken during the recap to distinguish between the family information and the trust information. In particular, the fact that Michael Tan is a child of Rebecca Tan and Jonathan Tan was recorded separately from the fact that Michael Tan is a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. Sophie Tan's inclusion in the discussion was confined to Sophie Tan's status as a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust. Adrian Koh's inclusion in the discussion was confined to Adrian Koh's status as a beneficiary of the Riverside Family Trust.

Mrs Tan was asked whether the summary accurately reflected the information she had intended to provide during the meeting. She confirmed that it did.

It was agreed that the information confirmed during the meeting would form the factual starting point for any further review. Any later consideration of matters not addressed during the meeting would require separate review rather than assumption. No substantive conclusion was reached about any matter beyond the factual information recorded above.

The meeting concluded with a final confirmation that the present exercise was intended to establish an accurate record of the relevant family and beneficiary position before further work is undertaken. Mrs Tan confirmed that this remained her objective.
