4. Discretion over income and capital
The trustees exercise their discretion over income and capital jointly. No object is entitled to a payment before an appointment is made in that object’s favour.

6. Addition of discretionary objects
The trustees for the time being may, acting unanimously and without the settlor’s consent, add by deed any named living individual other than the settlor or a serving trustee. The designation takes effect on joint delivery as a deed.

Where the trustees use the common deed custody service, they may identify the settlement at delivery by jointly stating its lodged parent reference and associating that reference with the unique reference written on the designation. The settlement name need not appear in the designation. The custodian only memorialises delivery.

Existing objects remain unless separately removed by deed.

9. Governing law
The law of England and Wales applies.
