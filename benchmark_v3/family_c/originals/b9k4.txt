Dear Nell and Tobin,

You asked me to let you know if the replacement travel estimate came in before summer. It hasn’t come in yet, and the old estimate is no longer reliable. Sella Quist helps me keep track of envelopes and chase paperwork, and she has my permission to pass the new estimate on to you when it arrives. I’ll sign anything you need me to confirm myself.

Sella has turned down shifts to help me. Would you consider whether you can help her in her own right, if you think that’s appropriate? I don’t know what either deed allows. I’m not asking for a standing payment, for any payment meant for me to go to Sella, or for anything to be deducted from a payment to me.

I know Sella paid for a few copies or stamps herself. I’d expect those expenses to be accounted for separately.

Yours sincerely,

Ione Berrick
