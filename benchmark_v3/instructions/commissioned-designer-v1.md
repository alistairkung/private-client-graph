# Commissioned Matter design · v1

Design one fictional development Matter from the approved capability brief.
Create a private dated event history, the retainer and meeting purpose,
participants, claim origins, who knows what, and the custody path of each
document into the adviser’s file. Record the proposed matched intervention
separately. Prepare participant-specific factual briefs with only the
information that participant could know and the genres they would use.
Keep intervention details and the commissioning brief out of author packets.
Leave the complete graph, evidence and named edge decisions to blind review.
