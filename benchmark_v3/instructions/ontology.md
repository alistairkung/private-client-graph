A graph contains named people and trusts. Score a relationship only when the file explicitly supports it at the stated cut-off. Use exact, distinguishable names. Local references and explicit cross-record identity links may resolve an endpoint.

Spouse and sibling relationships are symmetric. Common parentage alone does not establish the scored sibling relationship. Shared children alone do not establish marriage. Record other offices, administrative authority and unanswered questions in the companion register.
