# Six-family design checkpoint

Planning base is [PR 199](https://github.com/alistairkung/private-client-graph/pull/199)
at `ac85114ab842163877b5a3fb301fbc6864c4c449`.
The batch prepares M, P, R and Q under their registered revisions and
considers W and X under two new proposed revisions. W and X use previously
unallocated family IDs. Their [planning record](../rounds/2026-11-23-overnight-design.json)
contains general hypotheses and Architect provenance.

A design candidate uses the isolated packet runner and the existing
design inventory and graph contracts. The candidate profile resolves the
exact registered proposal, projects one capability brief, and requires a
design-only reservation. The commissioned profiles retain their existing
hypothesis approval checks. Candidate artifacts record provisional graph
and proof expectations for owner review. The factory derives adjudicated
keys independently from locked Sources during a later authorised
construction run.

The [PR 198 checkpoint](https://github.com/alistairkung/private-client-graph/pull/198)
binds design acceptance to the exact commissioned manifest and design
commitments. Candidate proposals and review artifacts convey no production
authority. Advancing a candidate requires an exact owner hypothesis decision,
a production manifest and budget, and digest-bound design acceptance at the
commissioned checkpoint.

The overnight run allocates two Architect turns and up to one isolated
designer turn per family. The private dispatch journal reserves each job
once, with at most two designers active. Rejected or uncertain submitted
turns count against the eight-session ceiling. A shared isolation or
integrity problem prevents new submissions. The owner-only review package
contains the design content, original responses, validations and cost
forecasts. Git contains the proposed planning record and candidate
validation only.
