# Benchmark v3 portfolio research scope

This custodian brief summarises the earlier v3 research specification and its
[packet protocol](../../docs/workflows/benchmark-v3-packets.md) for repeated
development planning. The initial specification defined three independent
Matter histories and eight conditions. A planning round may consider expansion
toward ten independent families. Keep conditions and observations separate
from Matter-family counts.

The benchmark asks whether a single full-context extraction recovers the
complete explicitly supported current Person/Trust graph at a dated meeting
cut-off, attaches the necessary passages to each critical edge, and withholds
named edges left open by the supplied file. Use six existing relationship
types, exact names and quotes, and the frozen v2 evidence policy and same-edge
sufficiency diagnostic. Unresolved questions are recorded by reviewers. The
extractor exposes only its graph and attached evidence for assessment.

Use wholly fictional text-readable Sources grounded in English and Welsh
document conventions. Put the ordinary meeting purpose and cut-off in the
adviser's file. Author documents from participant knowledge, derive the key
independently from the locked documents, audit in both directions, and require
dated human admission. Match variants by predeclaring the single allowed edit,
expected supported difference and unchanged material. Verify sufficiency by
whole-file masks, proper proof-set deletions, exact excerpt readers and
singleton checks. Challenge shortcuts based on dates, file order, first name,
similar-name merger, evidence union, administrative assertions and drafts.
Require a credible purpose and custody path for each record. A verified
contrast establishes a property of the authored file.

The v2 baseline motivated a shift from short, often explicit Sources to
documentary reconstruction. The v3 specification records 97.18% micro-F1
over 18 observations on 14 v2 conditions, a median of two Sources and 123.5
Source-body words, and an incomplete same-edge attachment for the v2 linked
target. A higher word count or lower score does not establish scientific value.
The [frozen v2 study design](https://github.com/alistairkung/private-client-graph/blob/c4a21e5f/benchmark_v2/README.md)
describes the control structure and scoring.

This is a pre-baseline v3 planning profile. Use admitted development technical
findings only. Keep model evaluation results, sealed data, OCR, Source
acquisition, retrieval, legal determinations outside the ontology, estimates
of workload prevalence, and practitioner outcomes outside the planning inputs.
