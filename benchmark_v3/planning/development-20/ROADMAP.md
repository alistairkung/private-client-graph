The stock factory's core cost for a family with (S) natural Sources and (C) scored conditions is (S + 4C + 2). This allocates independent design and audit, one author per Source and, for each condition, realism, two blind readings and adjudication. Literal and order siblings reuse natural originals through deterministic realization. Each section adds two isolated reading jobs per condition and leaves two whole-file readings in place. Mask count follows required risk and proof routes, with one independent assessment of each assignment. Coordination, owner time and subsequent Architect reconciliation need separate allowances. A five- or seven-Source pilot cannot identify the cost curve for giant files.

## Future operating decisions

- Start with two independent family workers and no simultaneous large packet backlog. Allow a third only after the owner reviews measured throughput, no unresolved isolation or integrity issues, independent-reader quality, complete keys and proof adequacy. Use one worker for each large or giant batch. Reusing already verified receipts cuts restart overhead and preserves independent role boundaries.
- Propose GPT‑6.2 Sol Medium for participant authoring and routine realism. Use GPT‑6.2 Sol High for design, annotation, adjudication, audit and pivotal masks. Allocate Medium to simple masks only when the independence and scientific check stay intact. The stock manifest assigns one model and effort per role per batch, so use the most demanding planned work to set each role or group families with similar needs. Recheck catalogue, effective configuration and observed receipt use before committing a production budget.
- Keep scope, authority, ledger and scientific quality checks at reservation. Use exact design and budget commitments before authoring. The #198 branch adds a design checkpoint to the #197 base. Adopt the reviewed checkpoint on the future execution base or use an owner-controlled hold at the same boundary. Break before authoring if a pilot overlap, named-identity incompatibility or ill-defined custody persists.
- Reserve bounded repair within each family ceiling. Default to one participant-safe natural documentary repair with exact impact tracing and dependent re-review. A material intervention, reflected in two or more bytes or facts whose effects cannot be isolated, goes back to the owner. A matched-sibling objection, hard budget increase or second repair needs new authority.
- Use deterministic commitments for literal edits, quote occurrence, reviewed inventory, graph diff and rerun reuse. An independent audit can precommit overlapping masks together when the same neutral packet truly tests the same dependency, with one clean sufficient-set observation and proper deletion coverage for each required route. Preserve two blind readings per condition, role isolation and a fresh admissibility review. Do not estimate a reduction by assuming proof jobs will be optional.
- Package each finished batch with actual reservations, attempts, tokens, unpriced costs, failures and disposition by family. Handle uncertain dispatch with the original receipt, and quarantine any unrecoverable or compromised lineage. A shared isolation issue halts submissions. Owner admission binds each exact dossier commitment. Then reconcile actual coverage, incremental value and failed operationalisation. Use the reconciled record and efficiency evidence to release the next batch.
- Stop where new files add mainly duplicate mechanism, arbitrary volume or unsupported legal inference. Also stop on unresolved independent disagreement, recurring realism failure in a document genre, insufficient proof, unexpected cost across batches, or a whole-file limit the approved runner cannot satisfy. Freeze a defensible sub-20 portfolio if needed.

The reviewed hypotheses estimate 2–5 owner hours per compact or moderate family, 4–7 for the large file and 6–10 for the giant, including design and admission checks. These are planning estimates. Retain additional custodian time for packet and receipt handling, monitoring blocked gates and audit readout, especially in large batches. The scientific ledger is denominated in sessions and reported tokens. Historical receipts do not establish a USD rate or an end-to-end elapsed-time SLA.

The chosen nominal Source count becomes an exact inventory requirement at commission. Check the semantic needs and body length before pinning it. Use the higher scenario for sizing follow-on review, with a separate owner budget decision before any increase. The stock factory stops at the approved family and task ceilings.

For the large tier, explicitly require the large-file audit in the admission criteria. The contract calls this tier `administrative`, while the stock automatic risk rule flags the `complex` and `giant` tags. A semantically large file needs independent evidence and full-inventory checks regardless of that tag. [Audit risk rules](../../batch/science.py)

## Release checkpoints

1. Close the separately authorised pilot through its own ledger. Require terminal dispositions, an owner review of the exact package summary, measured reservations and repair costs by role, and zero unresolved isolation, review-artifact or key defects. At least two scientifically useful admissions, including a genuinely tested matched family, would support the first measured expansion. Reconcile any admitted H/I/J and D/F, update the portfolio snapshot, and revise overlapping hypotheses before budget commitment. An owner continuation decision gates expansion.
2. Require the first new compact batch to preserve two blind readings, exact evidence and owner admission while tracking actual wall time, charges and retries. Release Q and S only if their marginal mechanisms survive technical review. Return or defer a weak design and revisit the total.
3. Close the 21–50 Source gap before U. The selected Q and S are nominally 17 and 18 Sources, with independently reviewed ranges extending to 22 and 23. If a real documentary need earns the additional Sources, revise the nominal inventory and budget and obtain owner approval. An independently defensible new intermediate case may also occupy one vacant slot. Require measured independent review with credible cross-Source joins and complete accounting before releasing U. Defer U and V if the gate stays open.
4. Commission a giant only after large-file timing, author, blind reading, adjudication, audit and owner burden have real measurements. Require separately approved giant sizing and budget. If approval stops or evidence gates fail, record the stop and freeze only the families the owner admits.
