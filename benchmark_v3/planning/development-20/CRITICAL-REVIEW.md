# Critical review and reasons to stop

## Portfolio-level findings

- **Scientific overlap.** Joint evidence, ambiguous links and at least one retained ordinary edge are common across the admitted corpus. They are safeguards in later cases. A new slot needs a different causal mechanism, a new diagnostic control or a workload comparison with restrained interpretation. A second switch contingent on a one-field lookup, a second release mechanism or a certified versus reporting-basis decision at the same scale needs a specific reason to survive.
- **Grounding.** Official guidance establishes record genres and points of handoff. It does not establish the proposed forms, chain of receipt, author knowledge, or relevance at that retainer. E is a concrete example of successful offline graph replay with insufficiently credible witness provenance. Reopen E only under separate owner authority and after independently establishing how comparable statements would enter that file. Avoid porting its history into a fresh ID.
- **Workload economics.** The pilot ceiling is a budget. The number of tracked Sources and the per-condition reading floor already rule out a flat 30-session projection at higher tiers. A higher number of independently authored Sources can make authoring the largest category while whole-file review drives latency and critical risk. The plan sizes both.
- **Ontology limits.** Supplied text can explicitly support six edge types. Parentage, trustee office and beneficiary status remain ordinary scoreable assertions where the file gives their meaning. Unresolved legal standing, classes without licensed named membership, executor, protector and other offices, title, capacity, property rights, validity and professional outcomes stay outside the scored graph. Exact homonyms without distinguishable permitted names can force a redesign or deferral.
- **Architecture.** The factory supports one literal body edit at a unique occurrence or a pure order change for stock matched conditions. Multiple changes, source addition, whole-series sequencing, and split-level factorial interventions need a separate diagnostic protocol if one existing literal edit cannot isolate the scientific question. Oversized full-file audit or adjudication is a resource and protocol question before it becomes an extractor design question.
- **Uncovered practice.** Retrieval from a DMS, OCR, handwriting and scan quality, external evidence collection, physical custody and chain-of-custody verification, legal adjudication, practitioner use and prevalence remain outside this supplied-text benchmark. Partial or unresolved coverage remains visible across admissions and later reconciliation.

Choose the next family by its mechanism and the defensibility of its evidence. Stop below twenty if independent design cannot make both sides of a comparison plausible, or if scaling would rely on arbitrary padding or thin review. A longer file earns its place when the underlying workload calls for it and the resulting scientific question survives review.
