**Plausible fictional conventions.** Intake inventories, references joining documents, neutral summaries, edition markers, handover correspondence, enquiry status and delivery logs require an issuer, purpose, recipient and credible custody path. Put any material authority or capacity distinction in an ordinary record that its author could possess. Count one received document as one Source under an inventory policy fixed before authoring. Retained copies or exports can be separately read Sources while sharing an origin.

**Unverified assumptions.** Source counts and lengths, tenure of advisers, density and survival of records, access to civil records, reporting cadence, and correspondence volume are design choices. Require a natural reason for each Source to be present at the meeting. Reuse genres across cases only where the research mechanism stays interpretable. Redact or design out irrelevant personal detail and avoid real client data.

**Representativeness.** No empirical frequency estimate or practitioner validation supports these allocations. Admission would establish that independent agent review reproduced the synthetic documentary mechanism. Retain uncertainty about how often such files occur, customary file sizes, and the consequences for practice.
