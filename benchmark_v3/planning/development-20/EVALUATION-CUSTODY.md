# Future evaluation allocation and custody

Reserve twenty independent histories in a separate evaluation namespace. The proposed distribution of 6 routine, 6 moderate, 6 complex, 1 large and 1 giant Matter gives evaluation its own routine anchors and enough mid-sized files to separate reasoning from workload. The giant allocation is conditional on its own context and review feasibility. Accept a smaller sealed portfolio if a capability has no defensible implementation or budget.

| Workload | Independent families | Coverage objective |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Routine, 3–8 Sources | 6 | Ordinary full-graph recovery, restraint and attribution in compact supplied files |
| Moderate, 9–20 | 6 | Independent exposures to temporal, authority, identity, contradiction and evidence-grounding difficulties |
| Complex, 21–50 | 6 | Multiple record functions, versions, incomplete information and interpretable combinations with simpler controls |
| Large, 51–99 | 1 | Workload and graph completeness under a natural administrative record |
| Giant, 100–200 | 1 | Full-file reconstruction and cross-section proof under a coherent supplied workload |

Counts are workload allocations and each family may cover multiple axes. The evaluation architect chooses genre mixtures, workflow allocation and experimental design independently. Ask the evaluation owner to check axis coverage before authoring and verified coverage after adjudication, using evaluation-only records. Select for different histories and broadly comparable difficulty within tiers, with several mechanisms per axis where feasible. Causal comparisons stay within lineage. Report matched conditions together and count the family as the independent unit.

## Restricted handoff

Use the stock `EvaluationBrief` shape and verify its workload counts and registered axes using the custody helper at future commissioning. Its `administrative` workload key represents the large band here. The policy and ontology arrive through separately approved, versioned public specifications. The detailed planning package is restricted from evaluation authoring and commissioning roles.

- Allow registered axis IDs, workload counts, broad objectives, approved six-predicate ontology and evidence/scoring policy.
- Exclude development family IDs, narratives, briefs, hypotheses, perturbations, histories, Sources, keys, review records and model results. Give the evaluation commissioner access only to the projection and the common policies. Pre-register evaluation coverage from that input before any evaluation construction.
- Use an owner-appointed evaluation custodian on a distinct OS account or isolated host, with disjoint private storage, credentials, ledger, signed lineage, role grants and backups. No shared worktree, connector, deployment secrets or mount to development material. Verify grants and negative filesystem checks on harmless sentinels. Revoke access and quarantine exposed lineages after an isolation incident.

## Admission and seal

Commission independent evaluation designers and participant-scoped authors in isolated packets. Precommit inventories and controls, then freeze the condition bytes and use two fresh blind readings per scored condition. Lock provisional keys before contrasts are disclosed. Require adjudication, per-Source completeness, critical evidence checks, risk-based masks, deterministic replay and custodian checks. An evaluation owner or delegated reviewer with evaluation-only access makes explicit decisions against exact dossier digests. Log returned and quarantined lineages separately.

Seal exact Source arrays, cut-offs, condition lineage, graph and evidence keys, policy and scorer versions, all reviewed hashes, and the task ledger. Store a signed and dated manifest and receipts in access-controlled, immutable custody with separate encrypted backup and audited access. Release only selected frozen inputs to an approved extraction environment. Keep truth and scoring on the custody side, and release only the metrics the evaluation protocol authorizes. Record access and freeze configuration, scoring and any diagnostic release policy before the first live evaluation. Any breach or salient corpus exposure requires a documented hold and a fresh lineage where needed.

The repository ships development commissioning and evaluation projection helpers. A separate evaluation execution environment and release/admission workflow require owner approval and runtime verification.
