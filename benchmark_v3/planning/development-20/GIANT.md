# Giant Matter feasibility and research design

Reserve one development lineage for a 100–200 Source file, provisionally sized at 150 Sources. An incoming private-client adviser reviews current trustee and expressly evidenced named beneficiary and family relationships before a handover meeting. The documentary workload is the reconstruction of a single family administration file spanning several years, several related settlements and multiple custodians. The meeting has an operational reason to review the earlier file, such as deciding which records support the current roster and which matters require follow-up. Dates, participants, trusts, documents, operative facts and keys belong to the independent design stage.

## Workload and research questions

Maintain one continuous administrative history. Use founding and amendment instruments, appointment and retirement records, minutes, correspondence across generations, annual accounts and reporting, and correspondence between outgoing and incoming custodians. Include naturally retained earlier records and references to unavailable enclosures where the meeting agenda justifies them. Give each Source a business purpose, issuer, intended recipient and plausible path into the file. Set a document boundary before counting. A single email thread with quoted messages may be one received Source with several origins. A separately retained attachment can be a separate Source. [Documentary grounding](PRACTICE.md)

A nominal 150-Source workload can be reviewed in approximately six sections. Section boundaries follow custodians, time windows or workstreams and may cross trust boundaries. The independently authored file should require some claims to be resolved across sections, including a join between an operative assertion and independently supported scope, identity or timing. Preserve ordinary local claims and unsupported questions for comparison. Joint proofs must exist because authentic records perform different jobs.

- **Primary full-file diagnostic.** Test whether cross-section critical claims have lower exact edge recovery or exact same-edge evidence sufficiency than predeclared local claims of similar predicate, proof-set size and naming difficulty. Equal or better cross-section recovery and sufficiency disconfirm this directional prediction for the file. Record differences in evidence distance and evidence difficulty. The groups are descriptive when they cannot be comparably selected.
- **Optional fixed-truth scaling.** Preselect a core at the same cut-off, ideally 10, 25, 50, 100 and 150 Sources. The core must already contain the complete scored graph and sufficient evidence for every critical claim, with stable entity roster and no authority change from additions. Omit any rung that cannot plausibly answer the full graph question. Independently verify the invariance and exact sufficient sets for each rung used. An evidence-neutral addition may be relevant business context. Match relative ordering and assess position or origin duplication separately where possible. A frequency or spread shift is part of this workload unless separately controlled. Test whether full-file recall or same-edge sufficiency declines as Sources are added, with clean additions predicting no graph delta. Equal or better recovery disconfirms a decline in this file. No extra scored conditions are allocated.
- **Optional evolving-history snapshots.** Freeze earlier meeting cut-offs with era-appropriate inventory and neutral briefs. Derive complete snapshot truth independently, including genuine changes. A change in recall or evidence sufficiency across these histories cannot identify a pure document-count effect. Treat snapshots as related diagnostics, budgeted and authorized separately.

The complete file remains the single proposed scored condition. Keep the fixed-truth and evolving-history series separate and label each as related observations from one family. The large-volume slot is the lower-cost opportunity to test whether an interpretable workload comparison can survive authorship and blind audit.

## Independent reconstruction

1. A custodian freezes the original Source bytes, ID, received inventory, dated scope, order, section assignments, cut-off and any diagnostic selection. Keep the design and hidden history in designer custody. The natural full-file key comes from actual supplied evidence.
2. Run two isolated reading tracks. Each independently reads every section and retains its own notes, exact excerpts and cross-section questions. Each receives the entire locked file and only its own section notes in a fresh final packet. The factory already implements separate per-track section reads and full-file reconciliation. Section labels and notes cannot dictate a target or circulate between tracks.
3. Compare the locked full-file outputs mechanically for reviewed Source IDs, exact text and canonical edges. The condition-local adjudicator reads both outputs and all Sources. Reconcile name variants using explicit cross-record evidence, verify current scope of older acts, audit overlap at boundaries and resolve every edge and evidence disagreement. An edge can require multiple sections. Each reader must independently seek missing cross-links and date/authority contradictions before adjudication.
4. Build a two-way completeness ledger. Every supplied Source receives a graph-support, contradiction, contextual, duplicate-origin or unresolved disposition. Every scored edge maps back to a sufficient set of exact quotes and original Source IDs at the cut-off. Retain date, origin and copy lineage in the review ledger and document the reason for exclusions. Preserve the supported and unresolved registers separately. Do not union fragments that concern different edges or treat a section note as a citable Source.
5. An independent audit checks both directions, ordinary invariants, cross-section joins, provenance and negative controls. Precommit isolated excerpt proof, proper deletions for joint sets, whole-file reverse coverage and shortcut tests. Deterministically validate citation occurrence, canonical identities, sufficient sets and complete Source inventory with the current replay and scorer. Evidence insufficiency, unresolved semantic disagreement, or lack of credible custody blocks admission. Owner admission binds the exact dossier and reviewed bytes.

## Capacity and resource gates

For 150 Sources, 250–600 words per Source gives about 37,500–90,000 words before framing. At 1.4–1.8 tokens per word this is roughly 53,000–162,000 input tokens from Source text alone. These are unverified sizing assumptions. Ask for separate estimates of schema, serialization, request overhead, both annotations in adjudication and audit, and output for graph plus exact Evidence. Reserve initial planning headroom of 20% of the applicable input-plus-output limit. Preflight longest packets for designer output, realism, two reconciliations, adjudication and audit. Measure actual serialized bytes and count tokens with the matching offline tokenizer or conservative documented proxy. Validate local transport, result sizes, response truncation and timeout handling against a deterministic stub.

The actual runtime and every job's output limit still need to be checked.
