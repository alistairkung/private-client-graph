# Wrenhollow Settlement
## Second Seat — Instrument of retirement and replacement appointment
**Executed-copy extract | 16 September 2026, 11:30 BST**  
**Fennick Vale Solicitors | Jules Neston**  
**Orrel Document Custody reference: OC-517**

### 1. Second Seat handover
Maren Voss, exercising the sole power under the Wrenhollow Settlement to appoint replacement trustees, appoints Tamsin Kell in place of Darren Brell in the Second Seat. Darren Brell retires from that seat and Tamsin Kell accepts appointment to it, in each case only upon delivery under clause 2. At that act alone, the retirement and appointment take effect simultaneously: Darren ceases to hold the Second Seat and Tamsin alone holds it in his place. Iona Brell’s First Seat is unaffected.

### 2. Escrow and delivery
Delivery of this instrument is expressly postponed. Perrin Saye of Orrel Document Custody holds the sole executed original under reference OC-517. Perrin Saye alone may lift the escrow restriction, and only by entering **RELEASED** against that sole original in the escrow control register. That entry, when permitted by the escrow controls, is the sole act of delivery.

**HOLD** prevents release. The time lock binds Perrin Saye, who must not enter **RELEASED** before the recorded earliest permitted time. The arrival of that time does not itself lift the restriction or deliver this instrument.

Signing the original and circulating this extract or any other copy do not constitute delivery or effect the handover. Perrin Saye may retain physical possession of the original after release; no physical transfer is required for delivery.

The original signatures and attestations are on file.
