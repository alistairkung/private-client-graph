Draft File Note – Ashby Family Trust
Dated: 18 May 2026
Status: DRAFT — NOT YET EXECUTED

I met with Gregory Ashby to discuss the future of the Ashby Family Trust. This note records both the current position and certain proposals that Mr. Ashby wishes to consider. It is important to distinguish the two.

**Current position:**
Gregory Ashby is the settlor of the Ashby Family Trust. He and his wife, Patricia Ashby, are spouses. They have two children, Emma Ashby and James Ashby. Emma Ashby is the current sole beneficiary of the Ashby Family Trust.

**Proposals under discussion (not yet in effect):**
Mr. Ashby has asked me to consider a variation to the trust. Under the proposed variation, James Ashby would become a beneficiary, and Emma Ashby's interest would be reduced. Mr. Ashby has also suggested that his sister, Margaret Ashby, should be appointed as a trustee in place of the current trustee. No deed of variation has been executed, and none of these proposals currently has legal effect.

Mr. Ashby confirmed that he has not signed anything, and that the current position remains as stated above until a formal deed is executed.
