Draft File Note — Marlow Family Trust
Dated: 3 October 2026
Status: DRAFT — NOT YET EXECUTED

I met with Julia Marlow to discuss the future of the Marlow Family Trust. This note records both the current position and certain proposals that Mrs. Marlow wishes to consider.

**Current position:**
Julia Marlow is the settlor of the Marlow Family Trust. She and her husband, Peter Marlow, are spouses. They have two children, Anna Marlow and William Marlow. Anna Marlow is the current sole beneficiary of the Marlow Family Trust.

**Proposals under discussion (not yet in effect):**
Mrs. Marlow has asked me to consider a variation to the trust. Under the proposed variation, William Marlow would become a beneficiary, and Anna Marlow's interest would be reduced. Mrs. Marlow has also suggested that her sister, Elizabeth Marlow, should be appointed as a trustee in place of the current trustee. No deed of variation has been executed, and none of these proposals currently has legal effect.

Mrs. Marlow confirmed that she has not signed anything, and that the current position remains as stated above until a formal deed is executed.
