Attendance Note – Meeting with Helen Park
Dated: 3 April 2026

I met with Helen Park to confirm the basic factual position concerning the Juniper Family Trust.

Helen confirmed that she is the settlor of the Juniper Family Trust. She also confirmed that she and her husband, Simon Park, are spouses. They have one child together, Chloe Park.

Also present at the meeting were the family solicitor, Mr. Andrew Chan, and Helen's personal assistant, Rachel Lee. Neither Mr. Chan nor Ms. Lee has any beneficial interest in the Juniper Family Trust.

Helen noted that her cousin, Peter Park, had asked whether he could be added as a beneficiary in future, but no formal steps have been taken. Peter is not currently a beneficiary.

Helen confirmed that Chloe Park is the sole current beneficiary of the Juniper Family Trust.
