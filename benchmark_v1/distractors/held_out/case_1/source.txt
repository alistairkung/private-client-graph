Attendance Note — Meeting with Thomas Reid
Dated: 9 August 2026

I met with Thomas Reid to confirm the factual position concerning the Stirling Family Trust.

Thomas confirmed that he is the settlor of the Stirling Family Trust. He also confirmed that he and his wife, Laura Reid, are spouses. They have one child together, Oliver Reid.

Also present at the meeting were the family accountant, Ms. Patricia Lam, and Thomas's business partner, Mr. Kenneth Ho. Neither Ms. Lam nor Mr. Ho has any beneficial interest in the Stirling Family Trust.

Thomas noted that his nephew, Daniel Reid, had asked whether he could be added as a beneficiary in future, but no formal steps have been taken. Daniel is not currently a beneficiary.

Thomas confirmed that Oliver Reid is the sole current beneficiary of the Stirling Family Trust.
