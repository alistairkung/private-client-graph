# Independent author packet

Assign one synthetic Matter per brief. Supply this packet, the supported PCG relationship and evidence contracts, the fixture format and the [authoring workflow](../docs/workflows/synthetic-case-authoring.md) to the author. Keep development examples, model outputs and other authors' drafts out of the authoring context.

| Brief | Decision the Matter must support |
| --- | --- |
| H1 · Authority | Resolve a current relationship using the Matter's documented authority rule when the most recent assertion points elsewhere. |
| H2 · Temporal reconciliation | Recover the entire graph as of a stated point. Require a decision about a superseded, corrected or future relationship while unrelated current facts still hold. |
| H3 · Cross-Source reasoning | Resolve a target from jointly necessary facts. Masking the linking fact throughout the bundle must leave at least two plausible endpoints. Identify the sufficient evidence sets for review. |
| H4 · Composite | Design one plausible private-client file that naturally combines multiple difficulties. Record what each intended decision requires and which conclusions are descriptive. |

Invent the family, documentary purposes, facts, chronology, relationship structure and placement of evidence independently. Work within Person, Trust and the supported family and trust relationships. Document the full answer key first. Write Sources, audit in both directions, approve exact quotations with explicit Source IDs, then derive an ideal and replay. Include all supported current edges and their evidence, critical required/forbidden edges and unaffected facts. Separate minimum sufficient sets on annotated targets from ordinary quote approvals. Provide the original file order and as-of point.

Deliver private packages to the independent reviewer. The reviewer checks realism, completeness, Source-specific evidence, diagnostic targeting and overlap with development and final families. Document which aspects of the narrative and reasoning establish independence. Record any prior exposure and reject derivative families before freeze. Author and reviewer sign separately. Return only opaque IDs, content commitments, review status and revision to the tuning team. Keep all documents, keys and ideals sealed until final configuration freeze.
