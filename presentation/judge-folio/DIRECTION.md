# Private Client Graph judge folio

Status: full production completed after the user approved the representative
rendered sample. The author's roadmap and methodology clarification, Private Wealth
category, simulated university-level judging feedback and requested Simplified
Chinese edition are incorporated. No new experiments were run.

## Format decision

The brief is one self-contained PDF below 20,000,000 bytes, not a slide deck.

| Format | Strength | Cost for this audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape slides | Immediate overview and strong individual images | Compresses methodological explanation; assumes narration |
| Editorial case study | Connects the intended user's problem to a tangible product | Needs disciplined research detail to avoid brochure language |
| Visual technical report | Accommodates controls, definitions and limitations | Risks burying the intended user beneath the experiment |
| Editorial hybrid | Product narrative with technical figures and an inspectable research chapter | Requires one consistent reading hierarchy across varied compositions |

Preferred format: **portrait editorial hybrid**, 720 x 900 PDF points (4:5).
This slightly wide portrait format supports readable prose and annotated product
details on a laptop. It is not a conventional slide canvas. Approximately 16-18
pages was the initial content budget. The finished English edition has 20 pages,
including the user's five-step methodology and twenty-scenario roadmap. The full
Simplified Chinese edition follows, giving one 40-page submission.

## Two concepts

### The reviewable Matter (preferred)

Begin with the professional's question. Follow a relationship from the graph to
its exact Source, explain proposal review and explicit whole-proposal acceptance,
then expose how the system is constructed and evaluated. The reader understands
the product before assessing its research evidence. This best addresses judging
of a working AI application.

### Anatomy of a claim

A visual white paper follows a single claim through documentary wording, model
interpretation, deterministic validation, professional inspection and benchmark
evaluation. It opens on the Bea/Beatrice failure. This gives scientific precision
and originality but asks the reader to care about an alias before understanding
the practitioner problem. Retain this as a focused investigation inside the
preferred narrative.

## Visual contract

Read mode with a persuasive purpose. The subject's signature is the visible link
between a relationship and its exact documentary words. Use that mechanism as
the principal graphic, rather than a generic network or AI illustration.

- White and cool chalk reading surfaces; forest green for major visual passages.
- Public Sans for explanatory prose and data; Newsreader Display for titles and
  quoted documentary language. The Chinese edition uses Noto Sans SC and Noto
  Serif SC. These are existing self-hosted project fonts. Original English
  documentary excerpts retain their English typography and exact wording.
- Body 16/23 pt; captions 11.5/16 pt; principal headings 36-54 pt.
- A 48 pt outer margin, 24 pt gutters, left-aligned prose, measured line lengths.
- Gold identifies documentary Evidence; burgundy identifies the recorded baseline.
- Authentic screenshots are visibly captioned as current local captures, historical
  workflow captures, curated fixtures, or retained model observations.
- No stock legal imagery, AI artwork, gradients, fake interfaces, or uncalibrated
  confidence gauges. Charts retain the full scale and exact denominators.
- Actual product screenshots remain unchanged. PDF clipping may select a detail;
  editorial figures and explanatory labels sit outside the captured pixels.

This inherits Forest & Chalk's product color language and the research site's
editorial typography. The earlier Evidence Folio art direction is a historical
reference, not evidence of the current deployed application. DESIGN.md stays
unchanged because this is a separate publication, not an application redesign.

## Final reading sequence

1. Product proposition and an exact source-backed relationship.
2. The original document-to-relationship problem and intended practitioner.
3. Why family links and Trust roles require distinct evidence.
4. The inspectable relationship: graph, Evidence and Source.
5. Synthetic multi-PDF intake and proposed Matter state.
6. Whole-proposal acceptance, discard and persisted current state.
7. Multi-source corroboration and the limits of current-state interpretation.
8. The semantic/deterministic boundary and why it matters.
9. Case hypothesis, development samples/truth, held-out set, Workbench measurement,
   then surrounding application support: the author's development method.
10. Separate held-out construction and ground-truth checks.
11. Relationship-level metrics and separate provenance diagnostics.
12. Frozen extraction and the A / A-prime / B experiment.
13. The two endpoint corrections and the resolver's enforced constraints.
14. Recorded aggregate results and actual Workbench chart.
15. Every held-out case's relationship F1.
16. Remaining evidence failures and additional provider usage.
17. What was demonstrated, what remains uncertain and reproducibility.
18. Governed future work and practitioner validation.
19. Twenty planned scenarios, five authored development cases, and the limited
    baseline-plus-resolver slice established within the hackathon.
20. Closing proposition, product/research/repository links and source index.

The same sequence repeats in Simplified Chinese on pages 21-40. Each cover gives
the language ranges, and PDF bookmarks provide two language roots. The judging
round moved the complete roadmap after the completed findings and strengthened
the early practitioner task, built-system summary and closing demo route.

The sample demonstrates cover, authentic product inspection, controlled
methodology and measured results. Its four pages are representative excerpts,
not the complete argument or the final pagination.

## Review principles

Applied repository frontend-design and Impeccable craft guidance (reading hierarchy,
subject-specific visual mechanism, restraint and bounded rendered review), plus
Apple Design's typography, color, accessibility and charting principles. UI-only
requirements such as responsive controls and animation do not apply to this PDF.
The user's explicit creative autonomy and single rendered checkpoint replace
the skills' additional interviews and concept-selection interfaces.
